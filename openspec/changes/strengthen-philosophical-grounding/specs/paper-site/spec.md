## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Every cited work is in the References
Every work the paper's body cites, by author and year or by title, SHALL appear in the References. The References SHALL group works as Philosophy, Psychology, Design, and Agents and evaluation, and SHALL order each group by the work's first publication.

#### Scenario: A citation is traced
- **WHEN** an author named in a parenthetical citation or with a title in the body is looked up in the References
- **THEN** an entry for that work is found

#### Scenario: A group is read
- **WHEN** the entries in one References group are read in order
- **THEN** their first-publication dates do not decrease
