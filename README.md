# Forgetting

Why we externalise memory, what kinds there are, and how
[particulars](https://github.com/nodelogicau/particulars) and
[intentions](https://github.com/nodelogicau/intentions) fit together.

People act best when their attention is on what is in front of them. These
formats hold what a person believes and what they mean to do outside the
head, with a record of where each thing came from, so that they can forget it
and trust that it is kept.

**[Read the whitepaper](whitepaper.md)** (draft).

This repository belongs to neither format. It holds the argument they share.
The specifications themselves stay in their own repositories, and nothing here
is normative.
